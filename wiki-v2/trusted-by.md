## Technical Partners and Solutions

Stellar Network occupies a central position in DOB Protocol's infrastructure, serving as the foundation for publishing verified metadata via DOB Validator and for DeFi operations through DobLink.

[Website](https://www.stellar.org/) | [Twitter](https://twitter.com/StellarOrg) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/stellar-development-foundation/)

### Soroban

BackOffice CMS, DOB Protocol's internal content management system, uses Soroban smart contracts to publish verified metadata directly on the Stellar blockchain. Each validated project gains an auditable cryptographic trail, transforming validation data into permanent and verifiable evidence. This approach eliminates information asymmetry between Project Owners and investors, establishing transparency from the start of the process.

Soroban, the Stellar network's smart contract platform, was developed to offer smart contract capabilities with low costs and high efficiency. Integration with BackOffice CMS ensures that all project validations and evidence are recorded permanently and verifiably, creating the trust foundation necessary for the Real World Assets tokenization process.

[Website](https://soroban.stellar.org/)

***

### Base

DOB Protocol's Investment Rounds and DEX operations run primarily on the Base blockchain, where DOB Token Studio Beta was launched. The platform enables tokenization and real-time distribution of revenues from tokenized projects, creating direct and transparent value flows.

Base is an Ethereum-derived Layer-2 blockchain that significantly reduces transaction costs while maintaining security and full compatibility with the EVM standard. Through the rollup mechanism, operations on Base indirectly leverage Ethereum network security.

[Website](https://base.org/) | [Twitter](https://twitter.com/base)

***

### Ethereum

DOB Protocol develops part of its technology in Solidity, maintaining full compatibility with the EVM (Ethereum Virtual Machine) standard established by Ethereum. Even while operating primarily on Base, DOB Protocol preserves compatibility that allows future expansion to other EVM-compatible networks.

[Website](https://ethereum.org/) | [Twitter (Ecosystem)](https://twitter.com/ethereum) | [Twitter (Foundation)](https://twitter.com/ethereumfndn)

### Uniswap

Using Uniswap v4 Hooks, DOB Protocol developed DOB Liquid RWA DEX, creating the first liquid DEX for Real World Assets. In December 2025, DOB Protocol was accepted into the Uniswap Hook Incubator.

Uniswap v4 Hooks enable programmatic and transparent liquidity management through smart contracts, making RWA tokens have integrated liquidity supported by real revenue flows.

[Website](https://uniswap.org/) | [Twitter](https://twitter.com/Uniswap) | [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/uniswap) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/uniswap)

## Legal and Compliance

### Ensuro Re Limited

DOB Protocol operations are conducted through Ensuro Re Limited, a legal entity that provides the regulatory structure necessary for statutory segregated accounts. Each tokenized project can have its assets linked to a specific segregated account within Ensuro Re Limited's structure, ensuring legal segregation of assets and liabilities and protection through Bermuda's SAC Act.

Ensuro Re Limited is incorporated in Bermuda (registered 2021) and holds licenses including Class IIGB (Innovative Insurer General Business), SAC certification, and DABA Class M.

[Website](https://ensuro.co/)

***

### BMA (Bermuda Monetary Authority)

DOB Protocol operations are conducted under Bermuda's regulatory context through BMA supervision. Frameworks such as the Regulatory Sandbox and Innovation Hub supported the development and graduation of Ensuro Re Limited.

[Website](https://www.bma.bm/)

***

### Bermuda

The regulated structure in Bermuda uses the SAC Act (Segregated Accounts Companies Act 2000) to ensure legal segregation of assets and liabilities of each tokenized project, providing statutory protection for segregated accounts.

[Website](https://www.gov.bm/)
