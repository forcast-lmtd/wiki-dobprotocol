## How does DOB Validator connect you with our Legal Framework?

The technical robustness of our architecture serves as an immutable foundation to guarantee the necessary legal security for RWA operations in the global market. Dob Validator acts as the regulatory gatekeeper of the DOB ecosystem, integrating natively with the structure operated by Ensuro Re Limited in Bermuda. Technical validation is an indispensable prerequisite for the legal protection required to enter the RWA market.

### DOB Validator: Structure and Protection

#### **Licensing and Jurisdiction (Bermuda)**

✅ Operation under Class IIGB and DABA Class M license.

✅ Exclusive jurisdiction of Bermuda courts, globally recognized for legal certainty in digital assets.

✅ Regulatory compliance built into the validation stage, eliminating subsequent legal friction.

#### **Asset Security (SAC Act)**

**Statutory Segregated Accounts:**&#x20;

✅ Each validated and tokenized project operates in a shielded legal silo.

**Asset Protection:**&#x20;

✅ Token holders' recourse is limited exclusively to the assets linked to that specific account, without contamination by the operator's corporate risk.

## From R\&D to Code that Becomes Product

Dob Validator is the practical application of years of academic research carried out by our co-founders Fernando Castillo (CTO), Oscar Castillo (CEO), and Simón Espínola (COO), with Fernando being the most active in these activities. DOB Protocol applies advanced concepts of trustless systems in its architecture and methods, producing mature solutions resilient to market challenges.

### Applied Concepts (From Paper to Product)

#### **Credential-Based Device Registration**

**Concept:**&#x20;

✅ Registration systems based on cryptographic credentials for authentication of devices and physical entities.

**Application:**&#x20;

✅ Verifiable credentials to authenticate Project Owners and physical assets, eliminating dependence on off-chain trusted intermediaries.

#### **Chained Verifiable Computations**

* **Concept:** ✅ Chaining of computations where the output of one step serves as cryptographic proof for the next.
* **Application:** ✅ The validation dimensions form a chain of trust (Existence → Operation → Revenue → Scoring). Each step generates an immutable proof anchored via Soroban smart contracts on Stellar.

**TrustOps & Evidence**

* **Concept:** ✅ Operations based on trustworthy evidence and auditable development.
* **Application:** ✅ More than 320 commits documented following rigorous transparency standards. The system provides cryptographic evidence of validation, eliminating information asymmetry.
