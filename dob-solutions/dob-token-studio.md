## How does DOB Token Studio connect you with our Legal Framework?

DOB Token Studio operates through the regulated structure of **Ensuro Re Limited** in Bermuda. Every Investment Round created through DOB Token Studio benefits from statutory segregated accounts, ensuring legal protection for both Project Owners and investors.

{% hint style="info" %}
**DOB Token Studio: Structure and Protection**

**Licensing and Jurisdiction (Bermuda)**

Operation under Class IIGB and DABA Class M licenses.

Exclusive jurisdiction of Bermuda courts, globally recognized for legal certainty in digital assets.

Regulatory compliance built into the tokenization process.

***

**Asset Security (SAC Act)**

**Statutory Segregated Accounts:**

Each Investment Round operates in a shielded legal silo.

**Asset Protection:**\
Token holders' recourse is limited exclusively to assets linked to that specific account, without contamination by operator's corporate risk.

***

**Limited Recourse**

Investors have rights to Account Assets of the segregated account backing their tokens.

No recourse to General Account assets or other segregated accounts.

Legal segregation guaranteed by Bermuda's SAC Act and anchored on-chain via DOB Validator metadata.

***

**KYC/AML and Compliance**

Identity verification integrated in Investment Round participation.

Prohibited jurisdictions automatically blocked (USA, OFAC, FATF).

Compliance with Bermuda (BMA) and international regulations.

## From R\&D to Code that Becomes Product

DOB Token Studio applies advanced concepts of _Revenue-Based Financing_ and _Tokenized Investment Rounds_ in its architecture. The final result is a Solution that eliminates financing barriers while preserving Project Owner control.

{% hint style="info" %}
#### Applied Concepts (From Paper to Product)

**Investment Round**

**Concept:** Tokenized funding mechanism where Project Owners open capital contributions on-chain through DOB Protocol.

**Application:** Each Investment Round defines token supply, revenue share percentage, and contribution limits. Investors receive tokens representing their share of future validated revenues. Investment Rounds operate through statutory segregated accounts, ensuring legal protection and asset isolation. The term replaces "pool" to focus on the investment and funding nature rather than technical DeFi terminology.

***

**Revenue-Based Financing**

**Concept:** Financing model where future validated revenues serve as collateral instead of traditional assets or equity.

**Application:** Project Owners commit a percentage of operational revenues to investors, creating alignment between project success and investor returns. DOB Validator ensures revenues are validated and distribution occurs automatically on-chain. This model eliminates predatory interest rates and preserves Project Owner control.

***

**Tokenization**

**Concept:** Process of converting real-world project revenues into digital tokens on blockchain.

**Application:** DOB Token Studio enables Project Owners to create Investment Rounds where each token represents a validated claim on future revenues. Tokenization provides fractional ownership, global accessibility, and transparent tracking of all distributions. All tokens operate within DOB Protocol's regulated structure through statutory segregated accounts.

***

**Execution Score**

**Concept:** Scoring system generated by DOB Validator based on auditable data across three dimensions: Project Owner Validation, Business Model Validation, and Asset Validation.

**Application:** The Execution Score determines project eligibility for tokenization through DOB Token Studio. Higher scores indicate stronger validation evidence and financeable project quality. Investors use Execution Scores to assess project risk before investment.

***

**Account Assets (Segregated Accounts)**

**Concept:** Assets linked to a specific segregated account within the SAC (Segregated Accounts Company) structure operated by Ensuro Re Limited in Bermuda.

**Application:** Each Investment Round created through DOB Token Studio operates through a statutory segregated account, ensuring investors have limited recourse exclusively to Account Assets of that specific account. This legal segregation is guaranteed by Bermuda's SAC Act and anchored on-chain via DOB Validator metadata, eliminating risk contamination between projects.
{% endhint %}
