## How does DOB WhiteLabel connect you with our Legal Framework?

DOB WhiteLabel offers direct access to **Ensuro Re Limited's regulated structure** in Bermuda. Financial agents operate through the same legal framework that guarantees protection for all parties, eliminating the need for complex and expensive proprietary legal structures.

{% hint style="info" %}
**DOB WhiteLabel: Structure and Protection**

**Licensing and Jurisdiction (Bermuda)**

Operation under shared Class IIGB and DABA Class M license.

Exclusive jurisdiction of Bermuda courts, globally recognized for legal security in digital assets.

Embedded regulatory compliance eliminating recurring legal costs.

***

**Asset Security (SAC Act)**

**Statutory Segregated Accounts:**

Each Investment Round operates in a legally shielded segregated account.

**Asset Protection:**

Account Assets exclusively linked to specific account, without cross-contamination between projects.

***

**Controlled Legal Costs**

**Structure Reuse:**

Validated legal templates reduce costs by up to 80%.

Growth without legal costs proportional to number of clients.
