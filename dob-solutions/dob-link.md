# DOB Link

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/16.png" alt="DOB Link"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

DOB Link solves the critical capital accessibility problem for tokenized projects. It allows Project Owners to embed investment widgets directly into their websites, creating frictionless capital flows while investors earn automatic double yield (real project revenue + DeFi returns) without manual intervention.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Main Features**

* Embeddable investment widgets enable direct capital access on any website.
* Automatic Double Yield: project revenues + DeFi returns via DeFindex on Stellar.
* Yields generated on Base automatically reinvested on Stellar without manual action.
* Eliminates accessibility barriers between projects and global investors.
* Native integration with DOB Validator ensures only validated projects access capital.
{% endhint %}

### How does DOB Link connect you with our Legal Framework?

DOB Link acts as the **regulated capital gateway** of the DOB ecosystem, natively integrating with the structure operated by **Ensuro Re Limited** in Bermuda. Every investment through a DOB Link widget flows through statutory segregated accounts, ensuring legal protection and automatic regulatory compliance.

{% hint style="info" %}
#### **DOB Link: Structure and Protection**



**Licensing and Jurisdiction (Bermuda)**



Operation under Class IIGB and DABA Class M licenses.

Exclusive jurisdiction of Bermuda courts, globally recognized for legal security in digital assets.

Regulatory compliance embedded in widget investment flow.



***



**Patrimonial Security (SAC Act)**



**Statutory Segregated Accounts:**

Each project accessed via DOB Link operates in a shielded legal silo.



**Asset Protection:**

Token holders' recourse is limited exclusively to assets linked to that specific account, without contamination by operator's corporate risk.



***



**Limited Recourse**



Investors have rights to Account Assets of the segregated account backing the specific project accessed via widget.

There is no recourse to General Account assets or other segregated accounts.

This legal segregation is guaranteed by Bermuda's SAC Act and anchored on-chain via DOB Validator metadata.



***



**KYC/AML and Compliance**

Identity verification integrated in widget investment flow.

Prohibited jurisdictions automatically blocked (USA, OFAC, FATF).

Compliance with Bermuda (BMA) and international regulations.

## From R\&D to Code that Becomes Product

DOB Link applies advanced concepts of _Double Yield_ and _Cross-Chain Capital Flows_ in its architecture. The final result is a Solution that maximizes capital efficiency while maintaining full regulatory compliance.

{% hint style="info" %}
#### Applied Concepts (From Paper to Product)



**Double Yield**



**Concept:** Mechanism that combines two simultaneous revenue sources for investors: (1) Real revenues generated by the tokenized project, validated through DOB Validator and distributed on-chain on Base; (2) DeFi returns generated by automatic reinvestment of these revenues in DeFindex on Stellar.



**Application:** The process is fully automated, eliminating the need for manual intervention and maximizing capital efficiency through continuous yield composition.



***



**DeFindex**



**Concept:** DeFi protocol on Stellar network where yields generated on Base are automatically reinvested through DOB Link.

**Application:** DeFindex allows investors to access multiple DeFi strategies without active management, creating the second return layer in the double yield mechanism. The DOB Link + DeFindex integration is automated via smart contracts, ensuring continuous capital flow between Base and Stellar.



***



**Embeddable Widget**



**Concept:** Optimized JavaScript code generated by the DOB Link platform that Project Owners embed on their websites to enable direct investment in tokenized projects.



**Application:** The widget is customizable (branding, colors, values), responsive (desktop and mobile), and embedded with regulatory compliance (KYC/AML, permitted jurisdictions). Each widget connects to tokenized Investment Rounds on Base, flowing through DOB Protocol's regulated legal structure.



***



**Cross-Chain Bridge**



**Concept:** Automated bridge between Base blockchain (where Investment Rounds are tokenized and revenues distributed) and Stellar blockchain (where yields are reinvested via DeFindex).



**Application:** The bridge is implemented via smart contracts that detect yield distributions on Base and automatically transfer funds to Stellar, executing reinvestment in DeFindex without manual intervention. Ensures continuous capital flow between two networks, maximizing efficiency and returns for investors.



***



**Account Assets (Segregated Accounts)**



**Concept:** Assets linked to a specific segregated account within the SAC (Segregated Accounts Company) structure operated by Ensuro Re Limited in Bermuda.



**Application:** In the context of DOB Link, each project accessed via widget operates through a statutory segregated account, ensuring investors have limited recourse exclusively to Account Assets of that specific account. This legal segregation is guaranteed by Bermuda's SAC Act and anchored on-chain via DOB Validator metadata, eliminating risk contamination between projects.


{% endhint %}
