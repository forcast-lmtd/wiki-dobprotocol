# Mission, Vision and Values

DOB Protocol was founded with a clear mission: democratize access to investments in real projects around the world.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Our Mission**

Enable anyone to invest in real projects anywhere in the world.

DOB Protocol provides financial services for real projects with measurable data, connecting global capital to real investment opportunities.
{% endhint %}

***

## Vision

DOB Protocol works to build an ecosystem where:

* Validated projects access global capital without traditional intermediaries
* Investors participate in real revenues generated by verified projects
* Project validation happens transparently and auditably on-chain
* Every economy participant can build real wealth through tangible assets

The vision is to create an RWA tokenization ecosystem connecting real projects to global capital markets, using blockchain to ensure transparency, liquidity, and legal protection.

***

## Purpose

{% hint style="info" %}
Build real and tangible wealth for families through real projects.

DOB Protocol exists to enable ordinary people to own stakes in productive systems, previously accessible only to large institutional investors. Each investment represents genuine ownership in real projects that generate verifiable returns.
{% endhint %}

## What We Pursue

DOB Protocol operates through five integrated solutions:

{% hint style="info" %}
#### Invest (DOB Liquid DEX)

Solution for the structural problem of illiquid RWAs on-chain:

* Integrated liquidity for RWA tokens via Uniswap v4 Hooks
* Investments starting at USD 10.00
* Flexible buy and sell based on project performance
* Democratized access to previously exclusive investments

***

#### Validate (DOB Validator)

Verifiably prove:

* The real existence of projects
* Continuous and functional operation of productive systems
* Revenue generated and distributed to investors

Each validation is recorded on-chain on Stellar Blockchain, creating an immutable audit trail that investors can independently verify.

***

#### Tokenize (DOB Token Studio)

Convert validated revenue flows into standardized tokens. Any Productive System can be served:

* SaaS platforms with monthly subscriptions
* Farms validating grain production
* Factories delivering products
* Mining fleets: GPUs and AI clusters
* Solar and renewable energy
* Electric vehicle charging networks
* Industrial robots
* Data and IoT infrastructure

Tokenization happens through future revenue, not through traditional equity or debt.

***

#### Distribute (DOB Link)

Connect tokenized projects to global investors:

* Embeddable widgets for Project Owner websites
* Distribution through DeFi and institutional channels
* Democratized access starting at USD 10.00

***

#### Integrate (DOB Whitelabel)

Modular plug-and-play solution for partners:

* APIs and SDKs ready for integration
* Customizable operational dashboards
* Complete tokenization infrastructure ready to use
* For Financial Agents and Development Teams
{% endhint %}
